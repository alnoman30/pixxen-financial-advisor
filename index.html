The four important things a prospect decides on your homepage

The best advisor websites let a visitor decide, without contacting you, whether they are the kind of client you work with. That saves your time and theirs, and it raises the quality of every call you take. The homepage is built around four decisions.

How are they paid?

Fee transparency

Build Trust With A Clear Fee Transparency Page

Fees are the question most prospects will not ask on a first call and most advisor websites refuse to answer. A dedicated fees page, in your words, is one of the strongest trust signals you can publish. It explains your model, what is included, what is not, and how fees are billed, with your Form ADV linked for the detail.

  • Clear explanation of your fee structure and billing model
  • What your fees include, exclude, and how they are billed
  • Direct access to your Form ADV for additional details

Compliance, disclosures & archiving built into the structure

Advisor websites are regulated marketing. The site is structured so your compliance review has clear places to work: a disclosures area in the footer and on relevant pages, links to Form ADV and Form CRS, a testimonials block that stays empty until compliance approves the required disclosures, and a content workflow that makes archiving straightforward. We do not publish anything you have not reviewed.

Gated guides & lead capture that qualify prospects

Not every visitor is ready to book a call. The ranking advisor sites offer a guide or checklist in exchange for an email: a tax planning guide for business owners, a retirement readiness checklist, an equity compensation primer. The site includes one lead magnet section and the email capture that goes with it, connected to the newsletter tool you already use.

  • Tax planning guides for business owners
  • Equity compensation primers for technology employees
  • Retirement readiness checklists for pre-retirees
